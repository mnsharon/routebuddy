# 🌿 RouteBuddy

### walk into moments, meet people along the way ✨

RouteBuddy is a soft, interactive web app that helps people connect through everyday movement — walking, biking, and pet walks.

Instead of just planning routes, RouteBuddy lets you discover *people nearby who are already moving*, making it easier to connect naturally and explore together.

---

## 💚 Features

- 🌿 Discover nearby walks, bike rides, and pet walks  
- 🗺️ Interactive map with real-time route markers  
- 🎯 Filter by activity (walk / bike / pet)  
- 🤝 Connect with people instantly  
- 🕒 View time & distance before joining  
- ✨ Soft, calm, and playful UI experience  

---

## 🧠 Idea Behind It

In a world full of apps that feel overwhelming, RouteBuddy is designed to feel:

> calm • breathable • human • intentional

It’s not about speed — it’s about connection.
